Notes

On repetition as cultivation

Spaced repetition is usually sold as efficiency — more retention per minute. But the better frame is horticultural. Review is watering. Each return to a card strengthens the root, and the schedule is simply the rain cycle. The goal is not to remember everything. It is to let the important things take root deeply enough to survive the droughts of neglect.

Zettelkasten in the age of language models

The zettelkasten was a technology for externalizing thought: capture a note, link it, let the network surprise you. Language models do not replace this practice; they change its economics. Retrieval becomes trivial, so the scarce resource is no longer *finding* an old idea but *deciding* which ideas deserve to be linked at all. The slip box survives because the bottleneck was never search. It was judgment.
